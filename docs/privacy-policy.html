Privacy Policy — Nawaitu

Package Name: com.tiarana.nawaitu
Effective Date: November 17, 2026

Nawaitu is a Muslim worship reminder and daily habit tracker application ("the App"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information on your device.

1. Data Collection and Storage

Nawaitu does not collect, store on external servers, sell, or share any personal or sensitive user data.

  • All worship schedules, reminder settings, and execution histories are stored locally on your device using an on-device SQLite database.
  • The App does not require user registration, login, or account creation.
  • The App does not use third-party advertising SDKs or behavioral analytics trackers.

2. Internet Access & Third-Party Services

Nawaitu uses internet access solely to fetch daily random hadith text from a public API (https://api.myquran.com) for display on the dashboard. No personal user identifiers or schedule data are transmitted in these requests.

3. Device Permissions

The App requests only the permissions required for its core reminder features:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) & Vibration (VIBRATE): To display and alert you when a scheduled worship reminder is due.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) & Boot Completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): To ensure worship reminders and snooze alerts fire accurately on time, including after device restarts.
  • Internet (INTERNET): To retrieve daily hadith content.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Since all schedule and history data lives exclusively on your device, you have full control over it. You can edit or delete individual schedules inside the App, or permanently remove all local data by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in your device settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect personal information from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the project repository: https://github.com/Naufalpc11/nawaitu-app.
